YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy information.

This website uses your account, patient and collection details to arrange lab services. Business data is handled by Forum’s API. Reports are private and are opened through short-lived authenticated links.

Preview mode uses fictional people and example slots. It does not send a booking or collect personal details.

In live mode, Google sign-in is provided through Supabase Auth. Your sign-in token stays in memory for the session. A temporary verifier is saved only while completing sign-in. Guest test selections and an unresolved booking reference may be kept in your browser’s session storage so a retry can be handled safely.

Hospital-approved privacy wording, retention details and contact information must be confirmed before public launch. This local version is not ready for public patient use.
