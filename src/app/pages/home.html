A LITTLE CLARITY

Before you begin.

Answers to a few common questions.

Can I book on the website today?

You can explore the booking journey in preview mode. No booking is created in preview. Live bookings require Forum’s API and sign-in configuration.

Do I need to pay online?

No. Payment is at collection, by cash or UPI to the phlebotomist.

Is home collection available in my area?

Availability depends on your pincode and collection slots. The website will check these with Forum’s API before you book; this preview cannot confirm serviceability.

Do I need to fast before my test?

Preparation depends on the test. Confirm the instructions with the lab when booking. Do not change medicines or diet without appropriate clinical advice.

How will I receive my report?

Released reports will be available through secure, short-lived links after sign-in. This preview does not contain patient reports.
