A LITTLE CLARITY

Before you begin.

Answers to a few common questions.

Can I book on the website today?

Yes. Choose your tests, enter your collection address and slot, and confirm your booking right here. You can track your visit and download reports from your account.

Do I need to pay online?

It is up to you. You can pay online securely via UPI, card, or netbanking, or choose to pay the phlebotomist by cash or UPI at collection.

Is home collection available in my area?

Enter your pincode or pick your location during checkout to check live home-collection availability and open time slots for your address.

Do I need to fast before my test?

Preparation depends on the test. Confirm the instructions with the lab when booking. Do not change medicines or diet without appropriate clinical advice.

How will I receive my report?

Released reports are available under Reports after you sign in, served through secure, short-lived links.
